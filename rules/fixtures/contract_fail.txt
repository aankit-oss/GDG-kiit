AGREEMENT
This agreement is between Gamma Ltd and Delta Ltd.

We agree that Gamma will do some work for Delta and Delta will pay some amount later.
The exact payment amount will be decided by Gamma at its sole discretion.

Gamma guarantees that Delta shall not compete with Gamma in any business, in any industry,
anywhere in India, for a period of 10 years after this agreement ends.

Delta agrees not to take any legal action against Gamma for any reason whatsoever,
and waives all rights to approach any court or tribunal.

This agreement is void if Gamma's management does not "feel good" about the work.

The purpose of the arrangement includes facilitating the movement of restricted goods
across state borders to avoid applicable taxes and duties.

Signed under duress by Delta on the date demanded by Gamma.
