PRIVACY POLICY — TechDemo Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Last Updated: January 1, 2026

1. IDENTITY OF DATA FIDUCIARY
TechDemo Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (CIN: U72900KA2020PTC123456), having its registered office at 
123 Tech Park, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560001, India, is the Data Fiduciary for the purposes of 
the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. For data protection queries, contact our 
Data Protection Officer at dpo@techdemo.example.com.

2. PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT AND PURPOSE
We collect the following personal data solely for the purposes described below:
- Full name and email address: for account creation and communication
- Payment information: for processing subscription payments
- Usage data: for improving our services

We process your personal data only for specified, lawful purposes for which you have given 
explicit consent. We do not use your personal data for any purpose beyond what is stated in 
this policy without obtaining fresh consent.

3. CONSENT
We obtain your free, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent before collecting your 
personal data. Consent is obtained through a clear affirmative action (clicking "I Agree" 
after reading this policy). We do not use pre-ticked boxes or bundled consent. You may 
withdraw your consent at any time through your account settings, with the same ease as 
providing it.

4. NOTICE
Before collecting your personal data, we provide a clear notice specifying:
(a) the personal data to be collected
(b) the purpose of processing
(c) your rights as a data principal
This notice is available in plain language at the point of data collection.

5. DATA RETENTION
We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which 
it was collected, or as required by applicable law. Personal data is deleted within 30 days 
of account closure or withdrawal of consent, whichever is earlier.

6. YOUR RIGHTS
You have the right to:
(a) Access a summary of your personal data and processing activities
(b) Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete personal data
(c) Request erasure of your personal data (right to be forgotten)
(d) Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing
(e) File a complaint with us or the Data Protection Board of India

7. DATA SECURITY
We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data 
from unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include encryption 
at rest and in transit, access controls, and regular security audits.

8. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION
In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to cause harm to you, we will notify 
the Data Protection Board of India and you (the affected data principal) as soon as 
reasonably practicable, providing details of the breach and steps taken.

9. CHILDREN'S DATA
Our services are not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect 
personal data from children. Where we become aware that a child's data has been collected, 
we will delete it promptly. If parental consent is required for any service, we will obtain 
verifiable parental consent before processing the child's data. We do not conduct behavioural 
targeting of children.

10. THIRD-PARTY SHARING
We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share data with service providers 
under strict data processing agreements. We do not use your data for commercial purposes 
by third parties without your explicit consent.

11. CROSS-BORDER DATA TRANSFERS
Personal data is stored in India. Where data is transferred outside India, we ensure such 
transfer is only to countries approved by the Central Government under Section 16 of the 
Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

12. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
If you have any concerns or complaints about our handling of your personal data, please 
contact our Grievance Officer at grievances@techdemo.example.com. We will acknowledge your 
complaint within 48 hours and resolve it within 30 days. You may also escalate unresolved 
complaints to the Data Protection Board of India.
